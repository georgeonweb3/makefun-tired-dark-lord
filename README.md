A ~100-word monologue for a fantasy villain who is secretly just very
tired — written for the
[make.fun bounty](https://make.fun/c/u-kevin-the-tired-dark-lord-a-100-word-villain-58b4f3cb).

He never says he is weary. The tiredness is in the rhythm: the wobbling
chair he has listened to since the hero's grandfather was a boy with a
wooden sword, the eclipse and the choir and the knife with the bone
handle — and then the roof leaked, the cellar flooded, and the ravens
stopped coming because the tower steps rotted through.

## Run

```sh
python3 main.py
```

Writes the monologue to `/output/entry.txt` (UTF-8, single line,
512 characters, 98 words — inside the competition's 1–600 character gate).

## The monologue

> You ask why I keep you alive. Sit down. No, the other chair — that one
> wobbles, and I have listened to it wobble since your grandfather was a
> boy with a wooden sword. I was going to unmake the sun. I had the
> eclipse, the choir, the knife with the bone handle. Then the roof
> leaked. Then the cellar flooded. Then the ravens stopped coming because
> the tower steps rotted through. Empires end the same way, hero. Not
> with a battle. With a draught under the door and nobody left who knows
> where the candles are kept.
